## Why this audit exists

Gates 0–16 have accumulated substantial reviewed structure without yet proving
the primary TUI learning loop as a composed product. Local correctness and
cross-document consistency are insufficient: every Gate can satisfy its own
contract while the assembled system remains rigid, unreachable, falsely
authoritative, or unable to improve a later learning move.

The audit therefore treats accepted ADRs, architecture, Gate contracts,
maintainer interpretations, tests, and close verdicts as falsifiable design
claims. Acceptance preserves the current decision and its provenance. It does
not turn that decision into a first principle.

## Reopened parent question

The audit did not begin merely because Gate 17 needed another contract review.
It began after ordinary discussion repeatedly collapsed distinct meanings:
starting a separate learning endeavor, continuing an existing Course,
continuing a Session, rebuilding bounded context, editing the next-Turn queue,
and steering one exact running Turn. That made it uncertain whether the current
documents and implementation could reconstruct Repa's product semantics
reliably or whether inherited vocabulary and accumulated Gate detail were
driving the interpretation.

The 2026-07-28 correction therefore restores the wider question: before
deriving Gate 17, is the current project a coherent and inspectable basis for
the intended Learning System? Closing that question requires all of the
following, not merely accepted fixes for already listed findings:

- current authority and status can be rebuilt without treating inherited
  OpenCode, preview-v2, historical review prose, or proximity to code as
  product truth;
- macro meanings that have different identity and lifecycle remain distinct,
  while the design does not manufacture separate machinery where no invariant
  requires it;
- the actual default TUI product composition and retained carriers correspond
  to the documented boundary;
- the current working tree is classified by causal change boundary, with no
  accidental generated residue, unexplained broad deletion, or unowned
  cross-Gate mutation hidden inside the corrective work; and
- the audit's healthy-boundary and completion claims are no broader than the
  evidence actually inspected.

This does not require settling Gate 17 or every future product choice. It does
require recording concrete remaining uncertainty instead of converting “no
new finding in the last review prompt” into “the project is healthy.”

## Falsification kernel

The following checks are derived from Repa's intended learning system and
general software-design constraints. They are audit criteria, not proposed
tables, modules, or mandatory workflow phases.

1. **Immediate learning value.** A learner can obtain useful orientation,
   explanation, demonstration, exploration, practice, or help from the primary
   natural-language TUI without first constructing a Course, Goal, plan, quiz,
   or other durable object.
2. **Long-running causal value.** Durable learning meaning earns its cost by
   changing a later context, teaching, review, planning, or correction decision.
   Storage, provenance, or a passing command test without a reachable consumer
   is not a product result.
3. **Truthful semantic ownership.** Meanings with different authorship, legal
   transitions, correction, or failure semantics do not share a false owner.
   Conversely, a conceptual distinction does not require a separate package,
   table, service, Gate, or transaction when one cohesive owner can preserve
   every invariant.
4. **Epistemic separation.** Learner intent, reports, observations, evidence,
   inference, curriculum, future attention, plans, and produced artifacts keep
   their actual basis. Persistence or model confidence never promotes one into
   another.
5. **Mixed-initiative control.** The learner can steer the immediate activity;
   models can perform open semantic work; the program owns trusted identity,
   closed arithmetic, legal transitions, permissions, and correction. Syntax,
   prompts, or deterministic policy must not impersonate semantic certainty.
6. **Causally complete correction.** Correcting an owning fact invalidates,
   recomputes, or truthfully preserves every dependent projection and plan.
   Append-only provenance is not enough if reachable behavior continues to use
   the invalid interpretation.
7. **Failure and recovery truth.** Interruption, provider failure, retry,
   restart, stale input, and partial multi-authority work cannot invent success,
   lose an already committed effect, duplicate it, or leave the learner unable
   to tell what actually changed.
8. **One product spine, several legitimate carriers.** The TUI is the primary
   product surface. Other retained interactive carriers may exist, but all
   converge on the same released-v1 Turn, model, command, context, and state
   owners rather than creating a shadow learning path.
9. **Bounded continuity.** One LearnerHome spans Sessions and Courses. Relevant
   exact state reaches a new or resumed interaction without eager transcript or
   database replay, while the current Session remains coherent and detail stays
   lazy.
10. **Inspectable consequence.** When a durable change can alter later
    behavior, the learner receives a truthful acknowledgement and can inspect
    and correct the owning meaning through the primary product surface.
    Valuable zero-write teaching needs no ritual audit.
11. **Compositional roadmap sufficiency.** Each producer has a reachable
    consumer, each consumer names its real prerequisites, and Gate composition
    closes the intended feedback loop. Linear numbering, independent reviews,
    or test volume cannot substitute for an end-to-end causal argument.
12. **Evolution without accidental rigidity.** The design commits only to
    distinctions required by current invariants and demonstrated consumers.
    It neither hides a universal ontology behind a broad noun nor freezes every
    current distinction into a permanent physical boundary.

## Required counterexamples

At minimum, the audit must try to construct these failures:

- all numbered Gates close while a fresh learner still encounters a generic
  coding agent or cannot complete one useful TUI learning loop;
- a durable fact is perfectly stored and reviewed but no later production
  sample consumes it;
- a visible prefix proves that a command was typed but does not resolve whether
  the learner meant a new Course, an existing route, a temporary activity, or
  no durable write;
- semantic separation is translated mechanically into many modules and
  cross-authority transactions, or broad “Agenda” language silently creates one
  universal owner;
- an accepted correction leaves a stale context, plan, acknowledgement, or
  fallback path reachable;
- a write commits, later provider prose fails, and the TUI reports neither the
  committed effect nor its correction path;
- TUI evidence passes while direct run, attach, server, or ACP bypasses the
  product spine, or carrier parity is mistaken for several Tutor runtimes; and
- exact provenance is used as a proxy for pedagogical usefulness, correctness,
  or a demonstrated later-action benefit.

## Finding disposition

Each finding must identify:

- the violated audit criterion and a concrete counterexample;
- whether the defect is in product intent, an accepted decision, architecture,
  Gate topology/contract, implementation, evidence, or documentation only;
- the narrowest causally complete repair and the stable outcome of materially
  wider or narrower alternatives;
- which closure or completion claims become stale; and
- whether the repair is derivable now or requires a maintainer-owned product
  trade-off.

No finding is closed merely by changing wording. No accepted Gate is reopened
merely because a document is old or a different decomposition looks cleaner.

## Confirmed findings

### `P17-F01`: Gate 16 implements natural-language semantics in SQLite

**Violated criteria:** truthful semantic ownership, mixed-initiative control,
failure/recovery truth, and evolution without accidental rigidity.

The Gate 16 contract says Goal entry is not restricted to `/goal` or any fixed
interaction shape, a clear fully learner-authored Goal may commit without a
redundant confirmation, and the runtime cannot prove full natural-language
semantics. Its implementation nevertheless makes
`learner_goal_commit_seal_direct_validate` recognize direct creation, cadence,
negation, scope, target, conditions, lifecycle, corrections, and replacement
through fixed English/Chinese substrings and templates. Direct updates and
replacements must also include the internal Goal ID in learner text.

Concrete counterexamples:

- `请记住我这学期要通过微积分` is an explicit durable learner outcome but
  misses the direct-create whitelist and is forced away from the accepted
  no-redundant-confirmation arm.
- `读懂小说 Every Day` contains a title that the trigger treats as cadence
  vocabulary.
- A learner cannot naturally say “把刚才那个目标改成……” unless their message
  also contains the implementation's internal Goal ID.

This is an implementation, evidence, and partial contract defect. The
structural Goal authority remains meaningful, but Gate 16 is reopened. The
narrow repair is not to add more phrases. Keep structural ownership,
foreign-key, uniqueness, version, append-only, transaction, and
application-fault invariants in the database; keep open semantic interpretation
in the model-assisted/application boundary; and test ordinary counterexamples
instead of only machine-shaped `/goal ... active LearnerHome goal with no
conditions and no target` fixtures. The historical close commit and reviewer
run remain provenance but no longer establish current acceptance.

### `P17-F02`: accepted documents promoted family names and provenance labels

**Violated criteria:** truthful semantic ownership, epistemic separation, and
evolution without accidental rigidity.

Normative documents used `Agenda` both as a family of distinct Goal,
future-attention, Assignment, planning, and possible detour meanings and as one
durable owner of all of them. The architecture also said source-grounded or
reviewed assertions had generically stronger planning authority after already
stating that provenance bases are not one confidence ladder. Neither conflict
requires a new product preference: different lifecycle owners remain distinct,
`Agenda` is only a family/composition label, and a provenance basis justifies
only the exact uses its source authority and consumer support.

The architecture, ADR-0012, native data model, and repository guidance now say
this directly. The same repair clarifies that policy precedence is
action/scope-specific, a context cut is not a universal stale-write guard, and
navigation continuity is not route progress. Gate 14 and Gate 15 are not
reopened by those wording corrections alone; focused implementation checks
remain pending where the contradictory wording could have shaped code.

### `P17-F03`: mandatory `/learn` does not own the ambiguity attributed to it

**Violated criteria:** immediate learning value, mixed-initiative control, and
evolution without accidental rigidity.

The prefix was accepted while discussing how to distinguish a new route from
continued learning and how not to interrupt a larger activity. It solves
neither problem. `/learn explain X` still authorizes no write; an exact
unprefixed `创建课程 X 并采用文件 Y` was forbidden from bootstrap even though
Goal, retained steering, and Artifact owners accept explicit natural-language
intent. The Gate 17 draft itself leaves new/continue to semantic
interpretation and explicitly excludes Session, queue/steer, macro activity,
and detour/rejoin ownership.

The inherited `.opencode/command/learn.md` used `/learn` to mean extracting
coding-session discoveries into `AGENTS.md`. It had no Repa product role and
was removed during corrective follow-through instead of being retained as a
permanent namespace constraint. Its former presence is evidence of inherited
configuration pollution, not evidence for or against any Repa control spelling.

The mandatory-prefix decision is reopened. This audit does not choose among a
mandatory coarse Course/View admission envelope, an optional discoverable
shortcut, or no special prefix. Any retained prefix must preserve trusted
cross-carrier provenance and be discoverable in the primary TUI; none may
replace domain authorization or semantic clarification.

### `P17-F04`: the future route could close without one product feedback loop

**Violated criteria:** long-running causal value, compositional roadmap
sufficiency, and inspectable consequence.

Under the former Gate 23 wording, Gate 19 could demonstrate an isolated
adaptation, Gate 20 could service an unrelated future-attention concern, Gate
21 could compute a static allocation, and Gate 23 could show them as orthogonal
traces. Every local acceptance could pass while no real learner outcome ever
changed a later Tutor move. Gate 21 also had no explicit production consumer,
and Gate 22 could report that “future behavior changed” merely because
candidate state existed.

Roadmap 09 now requires:

- one small connected causal trace from learning move through learner
  occurrence/outcome and an owned durable consequence to revised context/plan
  and a changed later Tutor move;
- exact later consumption of a Gate 21 allocation, including one changed input
  that changes the later move; and
- TUI separation among durable state, potential consequence, and actual or
  absent context/plan/action consumption.

This `P17-F04` repair does not by itself require a universal activity/outcome
table, a universal task-selection engine, or one giant all-feature test. The
later `P17-F16` finding separately owns representative move-selection and
re-entry responsibility.

### `P17-F05`: Gate 17 inherits an unsolved creation and partial-truth boundary

**Violated criteria:** failure/recovery truth, truthful semantic ownership, and
inspectable consequence.

Gate 8 deliberately did not prove Course creation because title, payload hash,
provider call ID, and a global revision are not honest semantic-effect
addresses. Gate 17 needs Course creation and may also produce View, Artifact,
Representation, or alignment consequences. If Course commit succeeds and a
later provider/domain step fails, blind retry can duplicate the Course or hide
the committed portion. The opposite blanket rule is also wrong: separate
domain ownership does not by itself require separate transactions. One
explicit local application operation may name, validate, authorize, and
atomically commit several consequences while preserving each owner's legal
transition.

The Gate 17 contract must therefore derive a request-bound creation/effect slot,
exact replay/conflict, and an explicit atomic-versus-staged composition rule.
Co-commit when one bounded local operation can know and validate every
consequence before commit. Stage work with a refreshed model context and
truthful partial receipts when it is external or long-running, cannot be
validated or authorized as one operation, or the next semantic decision
depends on the first result. Every staged path needs a durable TUI-visible
distinction among committed, already-applied, no-effect, failed, and
not-yet-attempted portions. This does not reopen Gate 8's
one-applied-mutation-per-immutable-context rule: a composite is one explicit
application operation, while a later distinct invocation requires a fresh
sample. A real bootstrap trace must compare the two shapes before a broader
mechanism is justified.

### `P17-F06`: later command families inverted the Gate 8 dependency

**Violated criteria:** truthful semantic ownership and evolution without
accidental rigidity.

The accepted shared-settlement contract is narrow: generic invocation,
trusted envelope, replay/conflict, receipt, and exact terminal settlement are
shared; each domain owns its effect identity and transition. The current
physical schema instead makes generic invocation/receipt tables enumerate six
command names, import every domain effect table, and carry mutually exclusive
nullable foreign keys for every effect kind. Gates 11, 14, 15, and 16 each
rebuilt the global tables to add another arm.

The original Gate 8 Course-command proof remains valid, but the later physical
extension is not a scalable implementation of its contract. Gate 8 is
scoped-reopened at that current physical dependency boundary. Before another
command family is added, generic invocation/receipt storage must stop importing
domain tables and accumulating effect columns. Domain-owned typed associations
may reference generic invocation/receipt while one application transaction
still commits the effect, receipt, terminal Tool Part, and required
projections. The exact physical mechanism remains an engineering decision; the
dependency direction does not.

### `P17-F07`: raw-SQL closure and mutable trigger installers exceed the real boundary

**Violated criteria:** failure/recovery truth, compositional roadmap
sufficiency, and evolution without accidental rigidity.

Gate 9 correctly states that SQLite integrity and foreign keys detect physical
and referential corruption while structurally valid out-of-band SQL is
unsupported. Gates 13–16 later promoted “arbitrary raw SQLite cannot forge
domain semantics” into closing evidence. Because an arbitrary SQL actor can
also drop triggers or replace the database, this is not an effective security
boundary. It culminated in Gate 16 duplicating natural-language and
acknowledgement semantics in triggers.

The trigger DDL is also not genuinely versioned:

- generated current schema does not contain the triggers;
- fresh initialization installs current `DatabaseSchemaExtras`;
- Gate 12–14 migrations import current constraint helpers, while Gate 15/16
  drop triggers and call the current aggregate installer;
- a database already at current `user_version` neither reinstalls nor compares
  trigger definitions; and
- upgrade tests construct an older-looking database by downgrading the current
  schema and reinstalling current helpers rather than loading a frozen
  historical fixture.

Changing a constraint helper without a migration therefore gives fresh and
existing databases different behavior at the same version while both pass
integrity and foreign-key checks. Gate 13's hostile-SQL evidence is
reclassified; Gate 14/15 product semantics remain intact, but their affected
constraint and migration-equivalence evidence requires correction. Gate 16 is
already reopened for a real behavior defect. Because migration lineage and
same-version admission belong to Gate 6, Gate 6 is scoped-reopened at this
boundary even though later Gates introduced the defect. The repair freezes
historical trigger DDL, versions every behavioral trigger change, tests a real
previous fixture, and keeps only structural application invariants in SQLite.

### `P17-F08`: the primary TUI cannot show what consequential approvals mean

**Violated criteria:** mixed-initiative control, inspectable consequence, and
failure/recovery truth.

Backend permissions carry exact metadata, but the primary TUI has no
learning/content-specific presenters:

- Gate 10 `content_mutation` includes exact canonical anchor, relative path,
  create/modify operation, lifetime, rights, and warning, yet the TUI displays
  only `Call tool content_mutation`;
- Gate 14's once-only default-Course confirmation displays only
  `Call tool set_default_course_preference`, not the exact from/to Course and
  View/Revision snapshot;
- Gate 16's accepted Goal candidate displays only
  `Call tool update_learner_goals`; and
- Gate 11's explicitly user-visible conversion result, Gate 14 route/default
  settlement, and successful Gate 8/10/15/16 tool results are generic and
  hidden by default rather than remaining truthful after a later provider
  failure.

The specialized permission and acknowledgement formatting exists only in the
direct-run carrier, and closing tests exercised that carrier rather than the
primary TUI. A user can therefore give a valid approval without seeing the
bound object, and a later provider failure can hide what already committed.
Gate 22's later general browser cannot repair a pre-commit authorization
failure.

Gate 8, Gate 10, Gate 11, Gate 14, and Gate 15 are scoped-reopened at their
affected primary-TUI implementation/evidence boundary; Gate 16 was already
reopened. Their domain identities and legal transitions remain valid. The
repair should provide one shared semantic presenter consumed by the TUI and any
retained carrier rather than copying another CLI-only formatter.

Implementation falsification extended the exact repair contract without
changing that disposition. A typed payload was insufficient while two
different Course revisions or Route Anchors could still render identically, or
while a retained-steering result collapsed to only state and version. The
accepted working-tree candidate therefore derives readable locators and result
meaning from exact owner snapshots, committed effects, predecessors, and
current heads; shows an opaque identity only when the readable locator cannot
disambiguate; persists one terminal semantic Part in the committing
transaction; and replays that stored historical acknowledgement rather than
recomputing it from current state. The original TUI reviewer closed the
envelope-binding, Goal completeness, canonical-content-anchor, scrolling,
exact-object, and retained-acknowledgement counterexamples. The later
`P17-F18` finding falsifies only the default-Course locator's same-name and
post-commit collision coverage; the typed visibility, transaction, replay, and
other supported locator repairs remain accepted evidence.

### `P17-F09`: Gate 5 still ships or emits upstream product identity

**Violated criteria:** truthful semantic ownership, one product spine, and
evolution without accidental rigidity.

Gate 5 explicitly removed hosted Web from automatic build and current release
participation while allowing harmless client source to hibernate. The
registered `packages/opencode/script/build.ts` does the opposite by default: it
builds `packages/app`, imports every generated asset into a file map, and adds
that map as a binary entrypoint. Only `--skip-embed-web-ui` produces the
terminal-only composition. The production server no longer exposes the Web
fallback, so the result is shipped dead composition rather than a second
interactive runtime. The primary TUI also still registers a visible
`Open docs` action that opens `https://opencode.ai/docs`, which presents an
upstream product surface as Repa help.

The defect is wider than build and help presentation. Config load and migration
actively insert `https://opencode.ai/config.json` and
`https://opencode.ai/tui.json` into Repa-owned files. Retained provider
transports send OpenCode referer, title, source, billing-origin, integration, or
user-agent values. These are reachable file writes and network claims, not
harmless internal `@opencode` package names. Gate 5 had deferred such headers
to a later identity audit, but independent-product identity is already a
current product invariant; the deferral was invalid.

Gate 5 is scoped-reopened at this active build, outward-identity, permission,
and product-surface boundary. The repair makes terminal-only the ordinary build
and requires an explicit non-release research target to include Web assets.
Runtime route disconnections, retained source, independently useful terminal
carriers, and internal namespaces remain valid. The misleading docs action must
be removed or point to a Repa-owned truthful help surface. Repa-owned config
must use a Repa-owned version-correct schema or omit `$schema`. Active network
metadata must identify Repa or be removed; a provider-specific literal may
remain only when the third-party contract demonstrably requires it and the
bounded interoperability exception is recorded and tested. Dormant
publish/install scripts that still target OpenCode identities are not active
release paths, but they remain explicit blockers to any future
release-readiness claim.

### `P17-F10`: custom-agent permission selection silently grants unknown capabilities

**Violated criteria:** mixed-initiative control, truthful semantic ownership,
and evolution without accidental rigidity.

The `agent create` command offers a fixed inherited list of permission keys and
writes `deny` only for unselected entries in that list. The runtime agent
default remains `"*": "allow"`. A learner who creates a custom agent selecting
only `read` therefore still grants every omitted learning/content capability,
including durable mutation tools added after that inherited list.

This is a Gate 5 retained-surface and generic harness
permission-composition defect, not a defect in each learning authority. The
repair must derive selectable keys from one authoritative
capability/permission catalog and represent a restricted custom agent as
default-deny plus explicit allows. A newly registered consequential capability
must consequently remain denied until deliberately admitted; a static UI list
cannot become an accidental authorization wildcard.

Implementation review found three additional ways the same boundary could
silently weaken an authored restriction:

- generated Agent identifiers could escape or collide with live catalog/file
  identities unless one grammar, canonical containment check, non-overwriting
  write, and live-ID collision rule owns the namespace;
- object-form permission keys that are ECMAScript array indices enumerate
  before authored string keys, so last-match evaluation can invert a deny and
  wildcard allow. Object-form capability and nested pattern keys therefore
  reject exactly canonical array indices `0` through `4294967294`; explicit
  ordered rule arrays retain numeric capability support; and
- raw JSON/JSONC plus ordinary assignment could discard an own `__proto__`
  deny before evaluation. Parsing and legacy compilation now preserve that own
  property without mutating `Object.prototype` or banning the capability name.

The original Gate 5 reviewer independently closed the generated-identity,
array-index, prototype-key, registry-visibility, default-deny, build, provider,
and outward-identity counterexamples in the working-tree candidate. These
findings widen `P17-F10`'s repair surface; they do not reopen every domain
permission contract or introduce a new Gate.

### `P17-F11`: soft workspace memory was written as current architecture without an owner

**Violated criteria:** long-running causal value, truthful semantic ownership,
and evolution without accidental rigidity.

The architecture described soft workspace memory as a normal Context
contribution with a host-bound path, digest, scope, loading reason, safety
policy, revision, and write receipt. No production mechanism, roadmap producer,
admission/correction owner, or demonstrated Gate 18 consumer supplies that
contract. Inherited instruction files and ordinary model-authored workspace
files do not satisfy it: they retain their existing machine-owned or
project-origin untrusted-content meanings.

The architecture now records this only as an optional future boundary that a
real consumer may earn. Gate 18 may project existing authorities and inherited
context mechanics but may not create a soft-memory subsystem merely to satisfy
old wording. This is a documentation/topology correction, not an order to
delete ordinary files or inherited instruction handling.

### `P17-F12`: Gate 23 could close without the product-floor teaching continuities

**Violated criteria:** immediate learning value, bounded continuity, and
compositional roadmap sufficiency.

The repaired roadmap requires one connected durable feedback trace, but Gate 23
could choose a future-attention or planning trace while two more basic product
behaviors remained uncomposed:

- Gate 17 could bootstrap a Course and teach; Gate 18 could separately pass
  projection fixtures; and a fresh Session could still fail to select and
  lazily read the exact recent Interaction/route state needed for a useful
  continuation. Gate 19 may legitimately shrink or defer, so its learner-record
  experiment cannot be assumed to supply this proof.
- A learner could say that an explanation did not help and receive the same
  explanation again. “Optional zero-write teaching” did not require feedback
  or correction to materially change the next peer teaching move.

This is a missing composition oracle, not evidence that Repa needs a universal
progress/activity owner. Gate 12 Interaction, Gate 14 route state, and Gate 18
lazy context are legitimate candidate producers for the first trace. The
second trace should remain zero-write unless some independently justified
later action needs durable meaning.

Roadmap 09 now makes both traces part of Gate 23's product-boundary evidence:
bootstrap and teaching must continue usefully in a fresh Session without eager
transcript replay or fabricated progress, and learner confusion or correction
