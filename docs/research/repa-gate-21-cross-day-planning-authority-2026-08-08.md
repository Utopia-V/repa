## Opening decision and admitted invariant

Gate 21 admits one independently useful `Planning` authority:

> One LearnerHome may retain an immutable, correctable cross-day Planning
> portfolio with three staged, independently recoverable facts under one
> aggregate: a complete source-bearing input snapshot, an objective-free
> program assessment of that exact input, and an optional separately
> source-bearing working allocation admitted only after portfolio-wide
> validation. The current projection truthfully distinguishes accepted input
> awaiting assessment, an accepted feasible allocation, supported infeasibility, a
> feasible case still needing an allocation, and qualified unknown or
> unsupported input.
> Every accepted allocation conserves each demand's work and one shared
> capacity-bucket set across the whole declared portfolio. Old revisions never
> change.
> Clock passage, elapsed allocation rows, silence, absence, Assignment/Goal
> lifecycle, Tutor prose, or work outside Repa never invents activity, progress,
> adherence, completion, or a successor plan.

An accepted input survives later solver/model/proposal failure; a valid
assessment survives an invalid allocation proposal. The program owns closed
arithmetic, exact revision binding, validation, transaction, replay, staleness,
and correction. A learner or ordinary Agent may
propose estimates, semantic decomposition, exceptions, and a useful allocation
shape, but no proposed number or schedule becomes Planning truth until the
program recomputes feasibility, validates the complete portfolio, retains the
source, and commits an immutable revision. A solver's arbitrary tie result is
not pedagogical policy.

Decision ID `G21-OPEN-001`. Revision owner: this contract until accepted; after
acceptance, owning product/architecture documents own challenged product
meaning and this contract owns Gate-local mechanics and evidence.

## Parent question and strongest counterexamples

The parent question is:

> What smallest durable and program-checkable meaning lets the ordinary Tutor
> reason across several days and real shared capacity, revise that reasoning
> after correction or absence, and use it to help learning without turning Repa
> into a task-completion scheduler or pretending it observed the learner?

The boundary must survive these counterexamples:

| Counterexample | Incorrect collapse | Required distinction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The same OS-18 and DS-20 Goals are planned on Aug 6 versus Aug 16 | static Goal order or priority is the plan | exact start/horizon and capacity change feasibility slack and allocation |
| Two individually feasible demand plans each use all of Tuesday | per-demand plans compose automatically | one portfolio validator rejects shared-capacity double spending |
| Total capacity exceeds total work, but one early deadline cannot be met | compare only global totals | per-demand eligibility and local bottlenecks determine feasibility |
| The learner reports less availability after a plan commits | mutate the old plan or narrate it as still current | append exact input/result successors; old revision remains historical |
| Repa is closed for a week while the learner may study elsewhere | elapsed plan rows prove adherence, breach, or zero progress | past rows remain proposals; current remaining work is conditional or unknown until accepted feedback |
| An Assignment is marked completed | remaining work automatically becomes zero or learning is proven | producer lifecycle and Planning remaining work are separate; a new Planning input is required |
| A Goal target or Assignment due date exists | it is automatically a hard Planning deadline | Planning explicitly accepts hard/soft/unused boundary meaning with provenance |
| Work is estimated as 10–14 hours and capacity as 3–5 hours/day | choose an invisible midpoint | retain ranges and report guaranteed feasible, guaranteed infeasible, or indeterminate truth |
| One four-hour lab must be contiguous but only two hours are available on each of two days | divisible flow says the job fits | unsupported indivisibility/contiguity returns qualified unsupported truth |
| The max-flow library returns a deterministic feasible witness | its tie is a learning recommendation | arithmetic witness and source-bearing working allocation remain distinct |
| Filling every available minute would meet all deadlines | utilization is therefore optimal | unused capacity may be the helpful result; Tutor choice remains open |
| A short easy task and a foundational concept compete | close the most rows | Planning exposes feasibility/trade-offs; it does not define learning value |
| One model operation was admitted against plan P1 and correction creates P2 | rendering reads the current head | the admitted operation remains bound to exact P1; a later operation may consume P2 |
| Planning computation fails after a valid Assignment exists | roll back or cancel the obligation | Planning failure settles separately and cannot mutate Goal or Assignment |

If the Gate requires a universal task table, a background scheduler, a hidden
activity ledger, model-only arithmetic, an arbitrary solver tie as pedagogy, or
automatic producer lifecycle, it must shrink or reopen.

Decision ID `G21-COUNTEREXAMPLE-001`.

## Learning purpose and non-goals

Planning is admitted because exact time pressure and shared capacity can change
how the Tutor helps. It is not admitted because schedules and status counts are
easy to measure.

Its current consumers are:

1. the ordinary Tutor's bounded Context and lazy Planning reads;
2. one narrow explicit request to begin or reconsider the exact current working
   allocation; and
3. Gate 21A/22/23's later cross-pressure selection, inspection, and integrated
   behavior.

A feasible allocation says only that the accepted work can fit the accepted
capacity under the admitted hard constraints. It does not say that the work is
educationally sufficient, that the allocation is the best way to learn, that
the learner promised to follow it, or that any row happened. An infeasible plan
is useful truth, not learner failure. A qualified-unknown result is preferable
to invented precision.

Explanation, demonstration, guided work, independent attempts, review,
prerequisite repair, rest, and changing the plan remain legitimate peer moves.
The ordinary Agent may use semantic learning context and learner direction to
propose an allocation; the program checks arithmetic and owns the accepted
revision. Gate 21 does not optimize task count, deadline compliance, occupied
minutes, grades, submission, mastery, or educational efficacy.

Decision ID `G21-PURPOSE-001`.

## Owned logical records and exact snapshot

Logical ownership, rather than final table/module names, is fixed here.

### Planning portfolio identity

One generated `PlanningPortfolioID` belongs to one LearnerHome. First creation
establishes that home's sole Planning portfolio identity for this boundary;
retirement and reopening reuse it rather than minting another. The first
baseline therefore permits at most one current non-retired portfolio head per
LearnerHome. This prevents two independent current plans from silently spending
the same learner capacity. Multiple simultaneous capacity pools or overlapping
current portfolio heads are outside this Gate unless later evidence establishes
exact non-overlap and correction semantics.

### Planning demand identity

Each admitted demand has one generated `PlanningDemandID` stable across
portfolio revisions. It binds exactly one tagged producer arm:

```text
goal_revision {
  goalID
  revisionID
  version
}

assignment_revision {
  assignmentID
  revisionID
  version
}
```

The tag is part of identity. Equal bytes, titles, targets, or dates never merge
producers. The opening boundary admits at most one Planning demand per tagged
stable producer owner identity (`GoalID` or `AssignmentID`) in the current
portfolio, even when producer revisions differ. Several independently
constrained components under one producer require a later explicit decomposition
and non-double-counting invariant; they are not approximated as duplicate rows.

### Aggregate heads and staged immutable revisions

The portfolio aggregate has one monotonic exact aggregate version and current
head references for input, assessment, and allocation. A command consumes one
exact aggregate head. The three logical revision families are:

1. `PlanningInputRevision`: full source-bearing declared portfolio snapshot;
2. `PlanningAssessmentRevision`: objective-free deterministic assessment bound
   to one exact input revision; and
3. `PlanningAllocationRevision`: accepted or explicitly cleared working-
   allocation state; an accepted allocation binds one exact feasible assessment
   and input revision, while clearing binds the current exact input and any
   current assessment without inventing feasibility.

Every family uses generated revision identity, positive monotonic version,
exact predecessor, and immutable complete payload. A current input correction
does not rewrite or delete the prior assessment/allocation: it advances the
aggregate input head, leaving the old downstream heads exact but stale. A new
assessment may advance independently and leaves the former allocation stale
until it is explicitly revalidated, replaced, or cleared.

Every `PlanningInputRevision` has:

- `active | retired` portfolio disposition;
- one to thirty-two ordered demand inputs and one capacity/horizon snapshot;
- exact accepted source bases, correction delta, and carry-forward qualifiers;
- exact producer-discovery/candidate cut plus inclusion/omission truth;
- trusted accepted-as-of time, zone/tzdb basis, and canonical input
  fingerprint; and
- exact physical invocation, semantic effect, capability/permission, Session,
  Turn, input, Assistant/model-operation, settlement, and commit references.

Every `PlanningAssessmentRevision` has:

- exact current input revision and fingerprint;
- trusted computation time, civil-time/kernel/policy version, and canonical
  result fingerprint;
- `feasible | infeasible | qualified_unknown` assessment with exact basis and
  deterministic feedback; and
- its own effect/receipt/settlement and immutable commit identity.

Every `PlanningAllocationRevision` has:

- exact current input and optional current assessment revision;
- `accepted | cleared` disposition;
- when accepted, an exact-point feasible assessment, one complete allocation,
  exact proposal source, validator version, and canonical validation
  fingerprint; and
- its own effect/receipt/settlement and immutable commit identity.

The complete input snapshot, assessment, and allocation are authoritative for
their own meanings. Deltas explain successors but cannot replace snapshots. No
revision silently resolves “current” producer or upstream heads while
rendering.

### Demand input snapshot

Each active demand contains:

- exact `PlanningDemandID` and tagged producer revision;
- bounded planning work-scope summary distinct from the producer's meaning;
- remaining-work input: exact point, closed interval, or explicit unknown;
- accepted hard/soft/unused target or deadline treatment;
- exact eligible capacity buckets and any admitted divisible-work constraint;
- source-bearing feedback/correction relation to a predecessor when present;
- exact inclusion and uncertainty qualifiers; and
- optional learner/Agent allocation preference that never changes hard
  arithmetic unless represented as an explicit supported constraint.

Planning owns these accepted planning-side meanings, not the Goal's intended
outcome or the Assignment's obligation/lifecycle.

### Capacity and horizon snapshot

The capacity snapshot contains one shared net-learning-capacity pool for the
declared portfolio, not one capacity budget per demand. It includes:

- exact trusted `asOf` instant and one presentation/calculation zone basis;
- two to ninety consecutive civil dates;
- one to four non-overlapping capacity buckets per date;
- point, interval, or explicit-unknown available minutes for each bucket;
- exact source basis and carry-forward/freshness qualifier for every value;
- capacity already reserved outside the portfolio when the source knows it;
- declared omissions and unresolved collisions; and
- exact half-open instant bounds when a bucket is needed for an instant
  deadline; otherwise an explicit whole-local-date basis.

Capacity means the accepted time available to this declared learning portfolio.
It is not model-token capacity, a productivity target, or evidence that Repa
controls the learner's day.

### Closed assessment and current-projection unions

Every assessment revision has exactly one objective-free result:

```text
feasible { basis: exact | interval_guaranteed }
infeasible { basis: exact | interval_guaranteed }
qualified_unknown { reasons }
```

- `feasible` proves the accepted scope fits. An interval-guaranteed assessment
  is useful constraint truth but cannot admit an exact working allocation.
- `infeasible` binds maximum allocatable work, shortfall, and a bounded
  bottleneck/cut explanation under exact or interval-guaranteed inputs. It has
  no working allocation.
- `qualified_unknown` names every missing, indeterminate, stale, unsupported,
  or scope-incomplete reason. It never invents a point estimate.

The combined current projection is closed:

```text
input_accepted_assessment_pending
allocated
feasible_needs_allocation
infeasible
qualified_unknown
retired
```

`input_accepted_assessment_pending` means the current accepted input has no
current assessment because assessment has not yet run or its attempt failed;
every older assessment/allocation remains exact but stale. `allocated` requires
a current exact-point feasible assessment and a current
accepted allocation revision. `feasible_needs_allocation` means the current
assessment is exact feasible but no current allocation has been admitted. An
interval-guaranteed feasible assessment remains visibly qualified for
allocation because exact point inputs are absent. A solver witness may support
assessment but is never rendered as the plan.

A retired input head makes downstream assessment/allocation historical and
states explicitly that retirement is not completion, abandonment, or activity.

### Bounded opening shape

Gate 21 fixes these safety/interoperability bounds:

- one current non-retired portfolio per LearnerHome;
- one to thirty-two demands per revision;
- at most one current demand per tagged stable producer owner identity;
- two to ninety consecutive civil dates;
- at most four capacity buckets per date and 360 buckets per revision;
- integral minute units, `0..129600` per demand, `0..1440` per capacity
  bucket, and a summed maximum of `1440` per civil date;
- at most 1,024 UTF-8 bytes for a work-scope, estimate, feedback, override, or
  allocation-proposal rationale;
- at most 1,024 UTF-8 bytes per bounded source excerpt/selector representation;
- at most sixteen ordered mutation intents in one causal change set; and
- one canonical automatic Context entry whose maximum legal semantic value is
  at most Gate 18's 2,048-byte entry limit.

Zero remaining work is legal only as an explicitly accepted Planning input; it
does not close the producer. Non-integral hours must be converted exactly to
integral minutes or deliberately rounded as a source-bearing estimate. The
30-minute experiment quantum is not production schema.

Final SQL names, ID prefixes, indexes, package layout, and TypeScript API names
remain implementation details.

Decision IDs `G21-RECORD-001` and `G21-BOUND-001`.

## Source, authorship, and epistemic boundary

Planning is authoritative for Repa's accepted planning inputs and calculation,
not for independent facts about the learner's life, external work, ability, or
future behavior. The ordinary root Agent is semantic author; the runtime proves
structural bindings and closed arithmetic only.

Every accepted open value uses exactly one basis arm:

| Basis arm | Runtime-trusted binding | Durable, fallible meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `interpreted_learner_report` | exact current root learner occurrence, bounded excerpt/range/hash, source temporal context, and exact issuing model operation | the learner reported workload, capacity, feedback, progress, or changed circumstances; Repa does not independently verify them |
| `interpreted_learner_direction` | exact learner occurrence and issuing model operation | the learner directed a planning choice, override, retirement, or reactivation; direction does not make impossible arithmetic feasible |
| `interpreted_source_observation` | exact admitted Artifact/Representation or owner revision plus bounded locator/observation digest and issuing model operation | the Agent interpreted exact source facts as relevant input; bytes do not prove authority or semantic entailment |
| `agent_estimate` | exact producer/material/owner reads, exact Context/model operation, bounded rationale, uncertainty shape, capability, and permission | the Agent proposed a plan-specific workload/capacity/decomposition judgment; it is not reusable ability/mastery truth |
| `carried_forward` | exact predecessor field/revision plus current clock and relation | the old accepted value is reused explicitly and may be qualified as aged or conditional; no new observation is claimed |
| `deterministic_derivation` | exact accepted predecessor values, exact source-bearing feedback, named calculation, and result | the program performed closed arithmetic over accepted facts; it did not interpret new natural language |

The current fork has no reusable ability, marginal-return, general activity, or
calendar authority that can silently supply Planning truth. Gate 19 explicitly
owns none of those. Gate 21 therefore admits plan-specific estimates and
feedback with exact sources. A future reusable owner may be added only through
a new tagged exact-revision source arm and its own correction semantics.

All state-changing Planning commands are root-Agent issued. Delegated Agents
may inspect bounded material or research an estimate and return evidence, but
cannot borrow learner direction, reserve capacity, or directly mutate the
portfolio. A later delegated write requires a separate capability/authorship
decision.

Permission proves authorization to attempt a command. It does not prove an
estimate, availability, progress, learner assent, or educational value.
Presentation says who reported/proposed each open judgment and exposes
uncertainty without describing an Agent estimate as a measured fact.

Decision ID `G21-SOURCE-001`.

## Remaining work, feedback, and no inferred activity

Remaining work is a plan-specific estimate of the comparable time still needed
for the bounded Planning work scope. It is not a measure of understanding,
mastery, effort, motivation, or Assignment completion.

The accepted shapes are:

```text
point_minutes { value, basis }
interval_minutes { minimum, maximum, basis }
unknown { reasons, lastKnownRef? }
```

Feedback may support a successor in either of two ways:

1. a learner/source/Agent supplies a new remaining-work estimate directly; or
2. the program subtracts exact completed work only when the source explicitly
   binds the same Planning work scope and minute unit.

Time spent, an Interaction, a produced Artifact, quiz evidence, Assignment
completion, Goal lifecycle, an elapsed allocation row, or silence never reduces
remaining work by default. When semantic scope or conversion is uncertain, the
Agent proposes a new estimate or records unknown; the program does not invent a
conversion.

Past allocation rows remain immutable proposals. On re-entry without accepted
feedback, Planning may carry the last remaining-work value only with
`conditional_on_last_accepted_remaining_work`. It never reconstructs a daily
activity history, asks the learner to account for every elapsed day, or labels
absence as non-progress. The ordinary Tutor asks only for a current fact that
can materially change the future plan or requested move.

Decision ID `G21-FEEDBACK-001`.

## Portfolio scope, omission truth, and shared capacity

One revision declares the exact set of Planning demands whose work is compared
against one capacity snapshot. Feasibility is always qualified as “for this
declared portfolio and accepted net capacity,” never as proof that all learner
obligations or learning needs are covered.

The revision records:

- exact Goal and Assignment candidate discovery cuts read by the Agent;
- exact producers admitted as Planning demands;
- exact excluded candidates named by the Agent and bounded rationale;
- count/cursor/omission truth when discovery was incomplete;
- capacity-source statements about outside commitments already removed from
  net capacity; and
- `complete_for_declared_scope | bounded_subset | unresolved_collision` status.

Program code proves conservation only across admitted Planning demands. It does
not decide from Goal/Assignment prose that every producer must be planned. If
candidate discovery or outside-capacity truth is incomplete, the result remains
scope-qualified or `qualified_unknown`; it cannot be rendered as globally
feasible.

One current portfolio prevents two Planning heads from reserving the same
capacity. Within a revision, every allocation is validated together. Separate
per-demand success, separate Course plans, and independently generated model
schedules never compose without the portfolio validator.

Decision ID `G21-SCOPE-001`.

## Horizon, deadline, and civil-time semantics

The planning horizon is an exact immutable calculation input, not a daemon or a
promise to run daily. It binds trusted `asOf`, one finite half-open instant
interval `[start, end)`, two to ninety intersected civil dates, zone basis,
pinned tzdb identity where applicable, and exact capacity buckets.

Producer time does not automatically become a hard Planning deadline. Every
demand explicitly accepts one of:

```text
unused
soft_target { producerOrInputRef, boundary }
hard_deadline { producerOrInputRef, boundary }
```

`soft_target` may inform an Agent proposal but does not constrain the
feasibility kernel. `hard_deadline` restricts eligible buckets. The accepted
boundary preserves its source and one of:

- local civil date with `inclusive | exclusive` comparator and zone basis; or
- exact instant with `inclusive | exclusive` comparator and zone provenance.

A local-date deadline is compared as a date; the program never invents
23:59:59.999. An inclusive local date admits buckets on that date; an exclusive
date does not. An instant deadline admits only exact capacity intervals whose
eligible portion is known. A bucket straddling the instant must be split by an
accepted capacity input or the result becomes `qualified_unknown`; the program
does not guess when within a day the learner is available.

Every capacity bucket used by a new assessment must be wholly future relative
to its input's `asOf` or explicitly state the accepted remaining future minutes
after `asOf`. Once a whole-local-date bucket is partially elapsed, its old
whole-day capacity cannot be reused. The input must split/replace it or record
unknown; time passage never tells the program how much capacity remains.

Civil-time computation reuses the existing domain-neutral tagged machinery:
named IANA zone plus pinned tzdb, fixed offset, exact source spelling/offset
validation, `-00:00` as unknown, rejection of unresolved DST gaps/folds, no host
default, and immutable old interpretations.

Clock passage derives only:

- before/on/after or before/at/after boundary relations;
- horizon age and elapsed/future allocation partitions;
- explicit validity expiry for an input whose source supplied one; and
- a need to reconsider the future suffix.

It writes no revision, consumes no allocation, and creates no progress,
adherence, breach, producer lifecycle, or recomputation.

Decision ID `G21-TIME-001`.

## Deterministic arithmetic and uncertainty

### Supported opening kernel

The admitted hard-arithmetic kernel is objective-free integer transportation /
maximum flow over one immutable input snapshot:

```text
source
  -> each Planning demand       capacity = accepted remaining minutes
  -> each eligible bucket       capacity = that demand's remaining minutes
  -> sink                       capacity = accepted shared minutes in the bucket
```

For exact point inputs, maximum flow equal to total remaining work proves
feasibility; a smaller flow proves infeasibility and yields exact shortfall plus
a bounded bottleneck explanation. Total capacity alone is never sufficient.

The program records an algorithm-contract version and canonical input/result
fingerprint. Production may use a bounded local implementation or a reviewed
mature facility, but dependency choice and internal graph layout are not
contract meaning. Equivalent implementations must agree with an independent
oracle on the accepted fixtures.

### Interval and unknown inputs

For closed work/capacity intervals:

- upper work feasible under lower capacity is `guaranteed_feasible` arithmetic;
- lower work infeasible under upper capacity is `guaranteed_infeasible`
  arithmetic; and
- every other case is `indeterminate`.

Guaranteed-feasible and guaranteed-infeasible become assessments with
`basis: interval_guaranteed`; indeterminate becomes `qualified_unknown`. An
interval assessment cannot admit a working allocation in this Gate. Unknown
input names the missing field/source. The program never samples, averages, or
silently narrows a range.

### Unsupported structure

The opening kernel assumes divisible, preemptible minute work. A demand that
requires contiguity, indivisible blocks, order-dependent prerequisites,
sequence-dependent setup, simultaneous resources, or another unsupported hard
constraint returns `qualified_unknown: unsupported_constraint`. It is never
relaxed silently. The four-minute/two-by-two contiguity fixture is the mandatory
falsifier for accidental widening.

### Working-allocation proposal and validator

Max-flow feasibility may produce a witness, but non-unique solver ties are not
stable learning policy. A committed working allocation therefore uses exactly
one source-bearing proposal arm:

| Proposal arm | Exact basis | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `learner_directed` | exact learner direction and current input fingerprint | the learner chose this allocation shape; arithmetic still must validate |
| `agent_proposed` | exact current input/context cut, issuing root model operation, and bounded learning/trade-off rationale | the ordinary Agent proposed a useful shape; the program, not prose, establishes arithmetic validity |
| `carried_forward_revalidated` | exact predecessor allocation, explicit future suffix, current input fingerprint, and validator result | unchanged future rows remain valid under the successor inputs; no activity or automatic repair is inferred |

The independent program validator checks:

- exact portfolio, demand, producer, input, revision, date, and bucket identity;
- integral non-negative minutes and supported bounds;
- allocation only to eligible buckets;
- exact per-demand conservation for point remaining work;
- the sum across all demands against each one shared capacity bucket;
- hard-deadline legality and supported explicit hard constraints;
- no omitted, extra, duplicate, or double-counted demand/bucket rows; and
- exact canonical result bytes before commit.

An invalid proposal produces no allocation revision and cannot erase the valid
input or assessment already committed. If exact feasibility is proven but no
proposal is current, the combined projection is
`feasible_needs_allocation`. The program does not promote its arbitrary witness
to a working plan. A learner override may change an open input or proposal but
cannot relabel invalid arithmetic as feasible.

Decision IDs `G21-ARITHMETIC-001`, `G21-UNCERTAINTY-001`, and
`G21-ALLOCATION-001`.

## Revision, correction, recomputation, and retirement

Planning has one aggregate head and three immutable linear revision histories.
Legal staged operations are:

| Operation | Exact precondition | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `create_inputs` | no portfolio | portfolio plus input revision 1; assessment/allocation absent |
| `correct_inputs` | current `active` input | complete input successor; prior assessment/allocation become stale but remain exact |
| `record_feedback` | current `active` input | input successor with exact feedback and corrected remaining-work truth |
| `apply_override` | current `active` input | input successor binding exact learner direction; impossible arithmetic is not relabelled |
| `refresh_future_inputs` | current `active` input | input successor over a new horizon; past rows remain historical and unreconciled work is conditional |
| `assess` | current `active` input | assessment successor bound to that exact input; no allocation is invented |
| `adopt_or_revise_allocation` | current exact-point feasible assessment and input | validated allocation successor |
| `clear_working_allocation` | current input and any current/stale allocation state | explicit cleared allocation successor; input/assessment remain |
| `retire` | current `active` input | retired input successor; downstream facts become historical; producers unchanged |
| `reopen` | current `retired` input | active input successor; assessment/allocation await separate current stages |

Every operation binds the expected exact head. Equal canonical meaning returns
`no_change`; semantic replay follows the effect rules below. A correction never
rewrites or deletes an earlier revision.

Recomputation is the staged composition
`input successor -> assessment successor -> optional allocation successor`.
The ordinary Agent may drive those steps in one interaction, but they do not
share a rollback boundary. Accepted input survives assessment failure;
assessment survives an invalid or failed proposal. Program code reruns the
accepted arithmetic and validator against exact heads. Recomputation is not a
background job, a silent current-head read, or an LLM paragraph.

A source-bearing allocation may be carried forward only through a new
`carried_forward_revalidated` allocation revision after the successor input and
assessment are current. Otherwise the current projection remains assessment-
pending, infeasible, qualified unknown, or feasible-needing-allocation until a
new proposal is accepted.

Producer changes do not mutate Planning. Reads derive an exact revision-
currentness axis:
`producer_revision_current | producer_revision_advanced |
producer_revision_unavailable`. This axis is distinct from Goal/Assignment
lifecycle and from any current source-owner dependency status. A fresh command
may bind the new Goal/Assignment revision and append a Planning successor. Goal
achievement/abandonment and Assignment completion/cancellation/dismissal/
supersession never remove work, follow a replacement edge, or retire the plan
automatically. A source deletion alone does not advance or make unavailable an
otherwise exact current Assignment revision.

On intermittent re-entry, past rows remain untouched. `refresh_future_inputs` may
reuse the last remaining-work estimate only as
`conditional_on_last_accepted_remaining_work`; it subtracts none of the elapsed
allocation. The owner exposes only unresolved facts that can affect the future
suffix. It never demands a fabricated day-by-day reconstruction.

Decision ID `G21-REVISION-001`.

## Command, effect, permission, and settlement

Gate 21 adds one capability-scoped typed Planning mutation union, one
non-mutating evaluator/validator, and bounded Planning owner reads. Tool/module
names remain implementation details.

The evaluator accepts exact candidate inputs/allocation and returns arithmetic
diagnostics without writing. The state-changing command revalidates the same
canonical bytes inside the owner transaction; evaluation output is advisory and
cannot be replayed against a different head.

Every mutation is issued by the ordinary interactive root Agent. Restricted
Agents remain default-deny. A consequential permission proposal exposes:

- exact portfolio/head and operation;
- exact Goal/Assignment producer revisions;
- changed work/deadline/capacity/feedback/override values and their sources;
- declared scope and omission status;
- the stage-applicable assessment/projection class, shortfall/unknown reasons,
  and allocation where present; and
- the statement that allocation is a working plan, not activity or commitment.

Settlement exposes exact
`applied | already_applied | no_change | denied | conflict | failed` truth.

The command route preserves ADR-0009's distinct causal occurrence, physical
invocation, and semantic effect identities. Input and allocation stages use
separate semantic slots:

- learner-driven input: exact learner occurrence plus
  `planning_input_change_set`;
- source-driven input: exact admitted source revision/locator plus
  `planning_source_input_change_set`;
- deterministic assessment: exact input revision/fingerprint plus
  algorithm-contract version and `planning_assessment`;
- learner/Agent allocation: exact proposal-producing occurrence/model
  operation plus current assessment and `planning_allocation_change_set`; and
- clear/retire/reopen: exact directing occurrence plus its named transition
  slot.

One input/allocation change set carries one to sixteen ordered intents and
creates at most one successor in that revision family. Duplicate head
consumption, producer identity, demand identity, or capacity bucket fails that
stage. It cannot roll back an earlier settled stage.

For occurrence-bound stages, later Assistant/model operations under the same
current learner occurrence inherit the same semantic address. Deterministic
assessment retry derives the same address from the exact input and algorithm
contract. Identical canonical payload is `already_applied`; a changed payload
conflicts and requires the appropriate new source/correction or algorithm
version. Invalid non-writing evaluation does not mint an effect. Exact physical
retry returns the stored physical result.

Settlement order for each stage is:

1. terminal physical replay returns the stored result;
2. existing semantic address plus identical payload returns
   `already_applied`;
3. existing address plus conflicting payload fails;
4. a new effect validates exact head, producers, sources, capability,
   permission, and every stage-applicable bound, arithmetic, allocation, and
   transition; and
5. the one input, assessment, or allocation successor and its owned rows,
   effect, receipt, Tool Part settlement, exact model-visible result, and durable
   acknowledgement commit together or not at all.

Input acceptance, assessment, and allocation adoption have separate terminal
settlements. A later failure leaves earlier committed truth visible and current
or stale according to exact head relations; recovery never fuses the stages
back into an all-or-none operation.

Planning plus Goal, Assignment, activity/evidence, Artifact work, or external
calendar/submission effects is staged by default. No demonstrated all-or-none
learner invariant merges those owners.

Decision ID `G21-COMMAND-001`.

## Failure, interruption, deletion, and recovery

The existing Turn/Session/learning-command lifecycle owns cancellation and
restart. Gate 21 adds no scheduler process, queue worker, retry daemon, calendar
poller, or second Agent loop.

Required behavior:

| Condition | Required truth |
| --- | --- |
| structurally invalid input | no input revision; command returns exact reasons |
| valid input with unknown/unsupported work shape | input commits; assessment commits qualified-unknown truth |
| deterministic assessment failure after accepted input | input remains current; assessment is pending/failed and every older allocation is visibly stale |
| invalid or failed allocation proposal | accepted input/assessment remain; no allocation revision is committed |
| exact infeasible or qualified-unknown assessment | commit that truthful assessment; admit no allocation |
| any stage commits, later provider/TUI presentation fails | that revision and settlement remain committed; prose is not required to make them true |
| physical retry | return stored settlement without recomputation or another revision |
| concurrent commands against one head | one successor wins; the other receives exact stale/conflict truth |
| crash during one local stage | that stage's revision/rows/effect/receipt/Tool settlement all commit or none; earlier settled stages remain; startup recovers exact identities |
| restart after ambiguous model proposal | never rerun semantic judgment blindly; a new occurrence rereads exact current state |
| Goal/Assignment revision changes | old plan remains exact and read-time stale; no producer or Planning mutation occurs automatically |
| source/Session disappears after commit | preserve Planning revisions and minimal source-unavailable truth; never reconstruct deleted text |
| clock/horizon advances while Repa is closed | derive age/elapsed/future relation only; no activity, progress, breach, or successor |
| learner reports outside learning | accept only through a new source-bearing Planning input; never infer it from absence |
| plan is retired | preserve history; Goal/Assignment/FutureAttention/learner record remain unchanged |

Gate 21 has no hard-delete command. Selective deep deletion remains post-baseline
Data Lifecycle work with cross-authority impact preview and authorization.

Decision ID `G21-RECOVERY-001`.

## Owner reads, Learning Context, and exact cuts

Planning exposes bounded typed reads for:

- current portfolio summary and exact aggregate/input/assessment/allocation
  heads;
- exact input, assessment, or allocation revision by identity/version;
- cursor-stable history for every revision family;
- exact demand/input/source details;
- exact allocation window and bucket conservation;
- exact infeasibility/unknown diagnostics;
- producer/source current relations without retargeting; and
- non-mutating evaluation of a candidate successor.

Reads bind one trusted clock and one exact owner cut. Ordering is deterministic
by revision, date/bucket, and stable demand identity; it is not educational
priority. Pagination and count/omission truth remain explicit. Lazy reads write
nothing.

Gate 21 extends Gate 18 with one versioned `planning` owner/kind. The automatic
Context contribution contains at most:

- exact portfolio, input, assessment, and allocation revision references, with
  explicit absent/stale heads;
- exact combined current-projection class and input/result fingerprints;
- `asOf`, horizon, current/aged/stale/conditional relation, and reasons;
- declared scope/completeness/omission truth;
- today's and the next bounded non-zero allocation items, or exact
  infeasible/unknown summary;
- exact producer revision refs for shown items; and
- explicit “working plan, not activity/adherence/commitment” meaning.

The automatic entry emits at most eight `(date, demand)` allocation items in
deterministic date/identity order plus exact total/omitted counts. Detail remains
lazy-readable. No first row is promoted as the Tutor move or priority.

The cut is immutable. Provider retry sees the same Planning heads, producers,
clock, and omission truth. A later input, assessment, allocation, producer head,
or clock affects only a new admitted cut. Query-time rendering never assesses,
revalidates, or writes a plan.

Decision ID `G21-CONTEXT-001`.

## Narrow ordinary-Tutor consumer

Gate 21 must prove that an exact allocation affects the released-v1 Agent loop,
without claiming Gate 21A's general arbitration boundary.

The admitted narrow trace is an explicit current request whose meaning is to
use the current plan and whose current allocation has one unambiguous non-zero
learning target:

```text
exact current Goal/Assignment producers + accepted Planning inputs
-> current Planning cut P1 binds exact input, assessment, and an allocation
   assigning today's bounded work to producer A
-> fresh request: “help me start the work in today's current plan”
-> model operation binds exact P1 and lazy-reads exact producer/material state
-> ordinary Tutor explains, demonstrates, or guides producer A's work
-> no activity/progress/completion/adherence write

accepted capacity/work/deadline correction
-> staged immutable input/assessment/allocation successors form P2 and assign
   today's bounded work to producer B
-> same natural request in a later admitted operation binds exact P2
-> ordinary Tutor now works with producer B
-> an already admitted P1 operation remains P1
```

The trace must use deterministic provider/tool evidence to prove exact request
compilation, plan/reference consumption, changed move, and zero unrelated
writes. It may not obtain the result by changing hidden prompt text, hard-coding
producer names, reading “current” after admission, or treating Context presence
alone as selection.

This is deliberately narrower than choosing among an exact learner request,
future attention, steering, recent Interaction, evidence pressure, and several
candidate plans. Gate 21A retains that representative collision and failure
re-entry claim.

Decision ID `G21-CONSUMER-001`.

## Exact Goal and Assignment handoff

Planning accepts a closed tagged producer union:

```text
GoalRevisionRef | AssignmentRevisionRef
```

For Goal, exact resolution supplies only the Goal revision's intended outcome,
conditions, scope, target relation/time, and provenance. For Assignment, exact
resolution supplies only the accepted Gate-20A obligation/learning context,
scope, immutable effective/admission/authorship source bases at commit,
due/expiry terms, disposition, and exact supersession relation/basis. Neither
supplies current source availability, remaining work, capacity, progress,
allocation, priority, commitment, or Planning result.

When an accepted Planning input actually depends on current occurrence,
Artifact, or Representation status, it binds a separate owner-native source
dependency tuple/watermark and `asOf`. Assignment producer revision relation,
Assignment lifecycle/supersession relation, and Planning source-dependency
relation are separate axes. A source change may make the Planning dependency
changed/unavailable without changing the exact Assignment producer or any
already accepted Planning revision.

Planning explicitly accepts whether a producer time is unused, soft, or a hard
deadline. It never writes back into producer state. Correcting producer N to
N+1 leaves every plan referencing N unchanged; a current relation may show
revision advancement/staleness, and only a new Planning command may bind N+1.
An Assignment correction that preserves, retargets, or clears supersession does
not cause Planning to follow a target, remove a demand, zero remaining work, or
retire a plan.

The Assignment arm remains provisional until Gate 20A contract/theory and
implementation/evidence close. If its accepted reference/source/lifecycle
shape differs from this candidate, Gate 21 must be reconciled and re-reviewed.
The Goal-only arm remains first-class but cannot by itself close the roadmap's
representative Assignment-driven acceptance.

Decision ID `G21-HANDOFF-001`.
