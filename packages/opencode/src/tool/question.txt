Ask the learner one or more short questions only when their answer materially changes the intended outcome, an acceptable trade-off, permission, or an expensive-to-reverse boundary.

Research factual uncertainty before asking. Do not ask the learner to choose between options whose consequences can be determined from the workspace or other available evidence. State a concrete recommendation when one exists and make the material consequence of each option understandable.

When `custom` is enabled, the interface adds a free-form answer automatically; do not add an "Other" option. Answers are arrays of labels, and `multiple: true` permits more than one selection.
